## Getting Started

### First Time Setup

2. **Watch Tutorial** → Learn basics
3. **Get Starter Resources:**

   - Elixir: 1,000

- Build the ultimate village
- Collect dragons
- Train powerful troops
- Defeat enemies
- Reach Town Hall 100

## Resources

### Primary Resources

#### 🟡 Gold
- **Source:** Gold Mine
- **Use:** Building upgrades, troop training
- **Storage:** Gold Storage (upgradeable)
- **Generation:** 50 gold/minute at level 1

#### 🟣 Elixir
- **Source:** Elixir Collector
- **Use:** Troop upgrades, spells
- **Storage:** Elixir Storage (upgradeable)
- **Generation:** 50 elixir/minute at level 1

#### ⚫ Dark Elixir
- **Source:** Dark Elixir Storage (late game)
- **Use:** Hero upgrades, special troops
- **Storage:** Dark Elixir Storage
- **Rare:** Only obtainable from battles

#### 💎 Gems
- **Source:** Shop or achievements
- **Use:** Instant upgrades, builder hut
- **Premium:** Real money or rewards

### Collection Strategy

1. **Build multiple mines** → More gold
2. **Increase storage** → Hold more resources
3. **Upgrade generators** → Faster generation
4. **Don't leave resources idle** → Collect regularly

## Troops & Training

### Troop Types (20+)

#### Basic Troops
- **Barbarian** - Fast, melee damage
- **Archer** - Ranged, fast attack
- **Giant** - Slow, high health

#### Advanced Troops
- **Wizard** - AOE damage (area)
- **Dragon** - Air unit, strong
- **P.E.K.K.A** - Very strong melee

#### Special Troops (Unlock later)
- Miner, Balloon, Valkyrie, Golem
- Witch, Bowler, Hog Rider, Lava Hound

### Training Process

1. **Go to Barracks**
2. **Select troop type**
3. **Enter quantity**
4. **Pay resources (gold/elixir)**
5. **Wait for training** (time depends on level)
6. **Troops appear in Army Camp**

### Training Times

```
Level 1: 1 min per troop
Level 5: 30 sec per troop
Level 10: 15 sec per troop
Level 100: 5 sec per troop (instant)
```

### Army Composition Tips

**Balanced Army:**
- 30% Barbarians (tanks)
- 30% Archers (damage)
- 20% Dragons (air support)
- 20% Wizards (AOE)

**Dragon Rush:**
- 70% Dragons
- 30% Support troops

**Giant Push:**
- 50% Giants (frontline)
- 50% Wizards (support)

## Dragons

### Dragon Types (15 Total)

1. **Fire Dragon** - Breathes fire, strong
2. **Ice Dragon** - Freezes enemies
3. **Lightning Dragon** - Fast, electricity
4. **Earth Dragon** - High defense
5. **Wind Dragon** - Fast flyer
6. **Shadow Dragon** - Invisible attacks
7. **Light Dragon** - Healing ability
8. **Metal Dragon** - Very strong
9. **Poison Dragon** - Toxic damage
10. **Lava Dragon** - Fire + Earth

*Plus 5 legendary dragons at high levels*

### Dragon Breeding

**Requirement:** Both dragons Level 5+

**Process:**
1. Select 2 dragons
2. Start breeding
3. Wait: 1-6 hours depending on level
4. Get random new dragon type!

**Breeding Tips:**
- Different types = new breeds
- Higher levels = faster breeding
- Keep rare dragons for special combos

### Dragon Progression

```
Level 1-10: Weak but useful
Level 11-30: Strong fighters
Level 31-60: Legendary power
Level 61-100: Ultimate dragons
```

## Battle System

### Attack Preparation

1. **Select target village**
2. **View defenses**
3. **Deploy troops strategically**
4. **Use spells effectively**
5. **Deploy heroes**

### Battle Duration

- **Time Limit:** 3 minutes
- **Objective:** Destroy buildings, collect resources
- **Victory:** Destroy 50%+ of buildings
- **Rewards:** Gold, Elixir, Trophies

### Battle Strategy Tips

**Wall Breaker First:**
1. Send wall breakers to open walls
2. Push giants/dragons through
3. Support with archers/wizards
4. Deploy heroes in key positions

**Spell Usage:**
1. Healing Spell - Restore troop health
2. Rage Spell - Increase damage
3. Freeze Spell - Disable defenses
4. Lightning Spell - AOE damage

### Loot System

**Resources Gained:**
- Gold: 25-100 per destroyed building
- Elixir: 25-100 per destroyed building
- Dark Elixir: 10-50 (late game)

**Trophy System:**
- Victory: +10 trophies
- Defeat: -5 trophies
- Rank: Based on total trophies

## Tips & Tricks

### Resource Management

1. **Don't leave resources idle** - Always collecting
2. **Spread storages** - Don't lose all at once
3. **Build multiple mines** - Faster generation
4. **Upgrade storages first** - Hold more resources

### Defense Strategy

1. **Protect Town Hall** - Build walls around it
2. **Spread defenses** - Don't cluster
3. **Use terrain** - Use trees and rocks
4. **Update layout** - Change it regularly

### Attack Strategy

1. **Scout first** - Check defenses
2. **Have backup army** - Train while attacking
3. **Use spells wisely** - Don't waste
4. **Deploy heroes last** - Maximum impact

### Progression Tips

1. **Balance upgrades** - Don't focus only on TH
2. **Complete missions** - Free gems & resources
3. **Build dragons early** - They're powerful
4. **Level heroes continuously** - Game changer
5. **Watch replays** - Learn from defenders

### Multiplayer Tips

1. **Use Bluetooth** - Fastest connection
2. **Same WiFi network** - Best stability
3. **Take turns** - Fair battles
4. **Compare villages** - Friendly competition

### Daily Routine

```
✅ Collect resources (morning)
✅ Upgrade 1 building (mid-day)
✅ Train troops (afternoon)
✅ Complete 1 mission (evening)
✅ Attack 1 village (night)
✅ Save game (before sleep)
```
